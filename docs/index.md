# Email Audit

Email Audit is a command-line tool for authorised email security assessments.
It reviews SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS and TLS-RPT, and can examine an email sample or
perform a controlled spoofing test.

The console shows discovered DNS records, explains their fields and highlights
relevant observations. The HTML report includes control assessments, an executive
summary, technical commentary and recommendations; findings are exported as JSON.

Start with [installation](installation.md), then follow the
[assessment workflow](workflow.md). For background, see the
[DNS and email primer](knowledge-base/dns-and-email.md) and [checks](checks/index.md).
